# Spoilers: the dark-matter entities ("the Unlit")

> Don't read this if you want the surprise.

## What they are

The Unlit are minds made of the dark sector: the invisible matter that makes up most of the
Coalescence's mass. They share only gravity with ordinary matter. As a result they cannot see
you, they cannot speak in light, and they can only touch your world by moving mass.

## How contact works

1. **Anomalies (Dusk).** Research *Halo Dynamics* and your astronomers notice some strange
   things: microlensing flickers where nothing is visible, planets precessing slightly wrong,
   and a knot of invisible mass moving against the flow of the halo.
2. **Gestures (Degenerate Age).** *Gravitic Semaphore* lets you answer by moving asteroids and
   moons. They reply with orbital perturbations that follow a numeric pattern. Your response
   costs energy and matter:
   - For a progression, *continue* it.
   - For a pattern that returns to its start, *reflect* it.
   - For a repetition, answer with *resonance*.
   - *Holding still* is always safe but teaches little.
   A wrong answer risks a misread. Their reply can shake a world, drop an asteroid on a
   settlement, or tear a planet loose from its star.
3. **The first reveal (Understanding 40).** The embers, the white dwarfs you harvest all
   through the Degenerate Age, stay warm because dark matter falls into them and annihilates.
   That dark matter is *them*. The power source your survival depends on is burning their kind.
4. **The second reveal (Understanding 70).** The falling is deliberate. For the whole life of
   the universe they have been invisible. Annihilating into light is the only way they can
   ever be seen, and they want to do it once, all together. The plan is to gather the last of
   the halo and burn it as a **dark star**: a star powered by dark-matter annihilation, the
   last new star there will ever be.
5. **Your choice.**
   - **Help:** give 200 matter and 100 energy, and do not use Halo Siphons. The Last Star
     ignites in the halo. It is a huge energy source for everyone still alive, and it restores
     some of the galaxy's free energy. Its light carries a message to everything that can see
     it, including a gift that makes the *Aeon Seed* achievable.
   - **Exploit:** build Halo Siphons, which drain them into the embers faster, or refuse them.
     They respond the only way they can. Invisible masses pass through your systems, tear
     planets loose and pull whole systems out of the galaxy.

## Physics notes

- The ember heating is real physics. Adams & Laughlin (1997) show that white dwarfs capturing
  WIMP dark matter stay at about 63 K and roughly 10¹⁵ W until the halo is depleted, around
  η ≈ 25.
- Dark stars are a real hypothesis for the *first* stars, which might have been powered by
  dark-matter annihilation (Freese, Spolyar and Gondolo 2008).
- A dark star in the far future, and dark-sector *minds*, are speculation. The game labels
  them as such.
