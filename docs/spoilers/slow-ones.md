# Spoilers: the Slow Ones' understanding

> Don't read this if you want the surprise.

The Slow Ones think around the Heart on a clock of millions of years. Deep Listening opens
contact (stage 1), they speak once our voices reach their clock (stage 2), and the player answers
their proof (`slow_first`): with mathematics (+22 understanding) or with our history (+15, and
120 insight banked); keeping silent sends them back to stage 1. Any answer puts them in
conversation (stage 3, "one thought per age"). At 45 understanding, while we can still converse,
they share the seam (stage 4, `slow_gift`): Conformal Mathematics costs 0.6 of its price, and the
Aeon Seed becomes possible.

**Until 19 Oct the gift could never come.** Understanding came only from the answer and from the
Last Light crossing (+15, if we were awake for it and they had spoken): 37 at most. Nothing else
gave any, since the first version of the code. The project check of 16 Oct found it (REPLICATE.md
S.1.3); the player asked for it to be reachable, without the details being told to them.

**Now:** the crossing that closes the Degenerate Age (the Great Decay, or the Last Warmth) gives
+15 too, on the same terms (the player only, awake, they have spoken): "We were awake as the
Degenerate Age ended, as the Slow Ones were. Another thought passed between us." So a
civilization that spoke with them before the Last Light reaches 52 (mathematics) or 45 (history)
as the Black Hole Age opens, when the Aeon Seed (built in that age) and Conformal Mathematics
are possible. One that first spoke with them later still reaches 37 at most: they noticed those
who were awake with them through both ends. Check: `tools/checks/unit/slow-ones.ts`.

Their painting (`public/art/slow.webp`: the Heart, its stars dimming and brightening in a
pattern), unused since the first plates, is on their card in Signals.
